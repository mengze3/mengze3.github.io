Spline Policy: A Structured Representation for Robot Policies

Mengze Tian†, Yiming Li†, Sichao Liu, Auke Ijspeert, Sylvain Calinon  ·  (under review)

We propose Spline Policy, a structured policy representation that models robot behaviors as spline trajectories rather than discrete action chunks, supporting flexible temporal evaluation, parameter-space constraints, and uncertainty propagation.

Locomotion

Learning Amphibious Locomotion for Salamander Robots

Mengze Tian†, Qiyuan Fu†, Chuanfang Ning, Javier Jia Jie Pey, Jonathan Arreguit, Andrea Ferrario, Auke Ijspeert  ·  (under review)

We learn a single reinforcement-learning policy for walking, swimming, and automatic transitions between them using proprioceptive feedback. A biologically inspired prior guides learning, while a system-level sim-to-real alignment pipeline accounts for sensing, actuation, mechanical backlash, and fluid interactions. We validate the policy on a physical salamander robot on flat and uneven terrain and in water.
